Health data in Zenith Fitness

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Zenith Fitness is a personal workout tracker. This page explains exactly what it does with the health data you allow it to read through Health Connect, and what it does not do.

What we read

Only if you grant permission, and only for the days shown in the Activity screen (the last 7 days):

  • Steps — daily totals.
  • Active calories and total calories burned — daily totals.
  • Heart rate — the daily average.
  • Resting heart rate — the daily average.
  • Sleep — the duration of each sleep session.
  • Exercise sessions — type, start time, duration and calories, so your workouts from other apps appear alongside the ones you log here.
  • Body weight — so your weight trend stays in step with your other apps.

What we write

Body weight only, and only weigh-ins you type into Zenith yourself. Nothing else is written to Health Connect. Workouts you finish in Zenith are stored inside Zenith, not pushed into Health Connect.

Where it goes

  • Health data is stored on your phone and in your own account in Zenith's database (Google Firebase, Firestore), so it survives a reinstall and shows on your other devices.
  • Nobody else can read it. Your gym, your training buddies and other Zenith users see only what you explicitly share with them (workouts and streaks) — never steps, sleep, heart rate or weight.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties or analytics services.
  • It is not used to train any AI model. If you ask Zen (the in-app coach) a question, only the summary numbers needed to answer it are sent to the model provider for that one request.

How to stop it

  • Turn the permissions off any time in Health Connect → App permissions → Zenith Fitness. Zenith falls back to manual entry immediately.
  • Turn off Settings → Health sync inside Zenith to stop recording finished workouts as activity sessions.
  • Deleting your Zenith account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes the stored health data with it.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the copy held on your phone.
