Zenith Fitness — Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026

Zenith Fitness is a workout, nutrition and gym-management app built and run by one person. This page explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how to delete it. It covers the Android app and the web app (zenith-fitness-18e2a.web.app) — both are the same product.

If you use guest mode, none of this applies beyond what's stored on your own phone: guest data never leaves your device, there is no account, and nothing here is collected or sent anywhere. Everything below describes what happens once you sign in with Google or an email code.

Account and profile

Signing in (Google, or a one-time code sent to your email) creates an account identified by a random ID. We store your name, email address, and profile photo (from your Google account, or one you set yourself), so the app and the people you connect with can show who you are.

Your workouts, plans and body data

Workouts, weekly plans, exercise notes, body weight and measurements, personal records and streaks are stored in your own account so they survive a reinstall and follow you to a new phone. Nobody but you can read them, unless you explicitly share a workout or a streak with a buddy.

Nutrition diary and food scanning

Meals you log, and any custom foods or meals you create, are stored the same way — in your own account. If you photograph a plate to estimate its nutrition, the photo is downscaled on your phone and sent to an AI model (Google Gemini, or OpenAI if Gemini is unavailable) to read what's on it. We do not keep a copy of that photo afterwards — it is used once, to answer that one scan, and discarded.

Health Connect (Android)

Only if you grant permission, and only for the days shown in the Activity screen (the last 7 days):

  • Steps — daily totals.
  • Active calories and total calories burned — daily totals.
  • Heart rate — the daily average.
  • Resting heart rate — the daily average.
  • Sleep — the duration of each sleep session.
  • Exercise sessions — type, start time, duration and calories, so your workouts from other apps appear alongside the ones you log here.
  • Body weight — so your weight trend stays in step with your other apps.

What we write: body weight only, and only weigh-ins you type into Zenith yourself. Nothing else is written to Health Connect. Workouts you finish in Zenith are stored inside Zenith, not pushed into Health Connect.

Where it goes: Health Connect data is stored on your phone and in your own account in Zenith's database (Google Firebase, Firestore), so it survives a reinstall and shows on your other devices. Nobody else can read it — your gym, your training buddies and other Zenith users see only what you explicitly share with them (workouts and streaks), never steps, sleep, heart rate or weight. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties or analytics services. If you ask Zen (the in-app coach) a question, only the summary numbers needed to answer it are sent to the model provider for that one request — not the raw Health Connect records, and it is not used to train any AI model.

How to stop it: turn the permissions off any time in Health Connect → App permissions → Zenith Fitness (the app falls back to manual entry immediately); turn off Settings → Health sync inside Zenith to stop recording finished workouts as activity sessions; delete your Zenith account to remove the stored health data with it; or uninstall the app to remove the copy held on your phone.

Zen, the in-app coach

Zen answers questions using your own training, activity and nutrition data. Each question you ask, plus a short summary of the data needed to answer it, is sent to Google Gemini, and to OpenAI if Gemini is unavailable, to generate a reply. Your chat history with Zen is kept on your device only — it is not stored in Zenith's database. Zen does not give medical advice or diagnoses; it will point you to a professional for pain or health concerns.

Location, for gym check-in

One check-in method reads your device's location, compares it to your gym's location, and stores only the distance in metres on your check-in record — never your coordinates. If your device or app can't get a location, or you'd rather not share it, your gym's daily front-desk code works the same way without it.

Push notifications

If you allow notifications, a device token is stored against your account so we can deliver them (new messages, buddy requests, gym announcements, and similar). It identifies your device for this purpose only.

Your gym

If you join a gym through Zenith, that gym's owner, managers and trainers can see your name, phone number, email, plan dates, payments and check-in history — but only for their own gym, not any other. Other members of the same gym can see your name and photo, and anything you post to the gym's feed. Feed and announcement photos are stored in Zenith's database and are visible to members of that gym.

Buddies and chat

Training buddies you add can see your workouts and streaks if you share them, chat with you, and see your badges. Buddy chat messages are stored in Zenith's database so they sync across your devices, and are readable only by the two people in that conversation.

Who we share data with

These are the outside services Zenith relies on, and exactly what each one gets:

  • Google Firebase — sign-in, database, app hosting and push notification delivery. This is Zenith's own backend, run on Google's infrastructure; Google does not use your data for its own purposes.
  • Vercel — hosts the small server functions (sign-in codes, push, account deletion, Zen, food scanning). Same role as Firebase: it runs our code, it doesn't use your data itself.
  • Google Gemini API — reads food-scan photos to estimate nutrition, and answers Zen coaching questions using the summary described above.
  • OpenAI API — a fallback for the same two things, used only when Gemini is unavailable.
  • EmailJS — sends the sign-in code to your email address when you choose email sign-in. It sees the email address and the code, nothing else.
  • GitHub — the Android app checks GitHub for the latest release tag so it can tell you an update is available. This call carries no personal data.

We do not run ads, do not use an advertising or analytics SDK, and do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

Security

Everything above travels encrypted (TLS) between your device and every service listed. Data at rest is encrypted using Google Cloud's standard encryption.

Children

Zenith Fitness is intended for users 18 and older and is not directed at children.

Your rights and deleting your account

You can delete your account any time from inside the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your private data (workouts, plans, body data, nutrition diary), your profile, your gym membership record, and your sign-in credentials. It does not reach into records that belong to someone else's copy of a shared thing — a message you sent in a chat, a post you made to a gym feed, or a payment your gym recorded — the same way deleting your own account elsewhere doesn't erase an email you sent from the recipient's inbox. Email us if you want any specific item like that removed by hand, and we will.

You can also just email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, without going through the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Material changes (a new third party, a new kind of data) will also be reflected in the app's own update notes.
