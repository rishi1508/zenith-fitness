## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Zenith Fitness is a workout tracker built for people who train on a schedule,
and for the gyms that run that training. It works as a plain fitness app for
anyone, and adds gym management tools when your gym signs up.

WHAT YOU CAN DO AS A MEMBER

- Log workouts against a weekly plan or your own exercise library: sets,
  reps, weight, RPE, and rest timers.
- Track body weight and measurements over time, with progress charts and
  personal records.
- Log meals from a food and recipe database, or photograph your plate for an
  estimated calorie and macro breakdown.
- Connect Health Connect on Android to bring in steps, heart rate, resting
  heart rate, sleep, calories burned and exercise sessions from your other
  fitness apps and wearables, and to keep a weight entry in sync both ways.
- Ask Zen, an in-app coach, about your own training, activity and nutrition
  history.
- Add training buddies, chat with them, and see each other's workouts and
  streaks.
- If your gym uses Zenith, check in with a QR code or the front-desk code,
  read gym announcements, and post to the gym's feed.
- Sign in with Google or a one-time email code, or use guest mode to keep
  everything on your phone only.

WHAT YOUR GYM GETS

- Membership, plan and payment records for your members, and a log of who
  checked in and when.
- Two check-in methods: a location-verified QR code, or a daily code your
  front desk rotates.
- A gym feed and announcements your members see inside the app.
- A view of who is training regularly and who has lapsed, without a
  spreadsheet.
- Premium member features (Health Connect syncing, camera food scanning,
  Zen coaching) turn on when your gym enables them. There is nothing for a
  member to buy inside the app.

Zenith Fitness has no ads. Your data is stored in your account, so it
survives a reinstall and follows you to a new phone. Health data you allow
it to read is used only to show you your own activity and to answer your own
questions to Zen. It is never sold and never used for advertising.

Privacy policy: https://zenith-fitness-18e2a.web.app/privacypolicy.html
```
