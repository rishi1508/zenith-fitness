Why the closed test is not optionalPersonal developer accounts created after November 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before Play allows a production release. Your pilot members are those testers. Start the clock the day verification clears and production lines up with the 1 October invoice.

One sideloading trapThe APK on GitHub is signed with our upload key; the Play build is signed with Google's key. A phone that installed the APK cannot update to the Play build in place — it must uninstall first (data is in the cloud). So: members wait for the Play link, only you and the trainers keep using the APK meanwhile.
