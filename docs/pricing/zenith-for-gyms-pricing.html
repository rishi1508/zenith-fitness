What every tier includes

For the front desk and the owner

  • Members, plans, payments and renewal reminders with a UPI link
  • Check-in console: daily code, poster QR, member QR, manual
  • Classes, enrolment and attendance
  • Analytics: MRR, churn, members slipping away, peak hours, revenue split, trainer utilisation, equipment health
  • Anonymous session ratings by trainer and class
  • Announcements to every member's phone
  • Staff accounts for trainers and managers, no seat limit

For every member, at no charge to them

  • Zenith Premium: Zen coach, plate scan, insights, weight and energy tracking
  • Your gym's exercises with your trainers' videos, playable mid-workout
  • Your gym's workout plans, adopted with one tap
  • Gym feed, buddies, streaks, badges
  • Health Connect sync on Android
  • Your gym's name and logo on the gym tab and home screen

How we count, bill and change tiers

Member
A person on your roster whose membership is active, or lapsed less than 7 days, on the 1st of the month. Staff and test entries are not counted.
Outgrowing a tier
If the count on the 1st is above your cap, the next tier applies from the following month. No mid-month changes and no back-charges.
Shrinking
If the count falls under a lower cap for two consecutive months, you may move down at the next monthly renewal.
Billing
Monthly in advance by UPI or bank transfer, invoice sent on the 1st, due in 7 days. Annual plans are invoiced once.
Price changes
At most once a year, capped at 10%, with 60 days' written notice. Annual plans are locked for their term.
Leaving
Cancel any time with 7 days' notice; the current month is not refunded. Your member list and payment history are exported to you on request. Members keep their own training data in Zenith.
